Our Services

From Groundwork to Final Finish

We deliver strong, reliable civil construction services.

Preparatory & Clearing Work

Site preparation, clearing, layout support, and initial groundwork before construction starts.

Earth Work in Excavation

Excavation, soil cutting, filling, and level preparation for strong civil foundations.

Stone Masonry, Bridge & Culvert

Stone masonry work, bridge support, and culvert construction for durable infrastructure.

Granular Sub-Base Work

GSB layer preparation and compaction for reliable road and pavement base strength.

WMM & WBM Work

Wet mix macadam and water bound macadam work with proper grading and compaction.

HYSD Bar Reinforcement

Supplying, fitting, and placing HYSD bar reinforcement for RCC structural strength.

PCC & RCC Work

Plain cement concrete and reinforced cement concrete work for foundations and structures.

SDBC Grading-II

Semi dense bituminous concrete grading work for smooth, durable road surfacing.

Bituminous Macadam Work

Bituminous macadam laying and compaction for strong road base and surface quality.

Prime Coat & Tack Coat

Prime coat and tack coat application for proper bonding between road layers.

Finishing Work

Final finishing, checking, cleaning, and completion work for ready-to-use project delivery.
